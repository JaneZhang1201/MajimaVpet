Opening it the first time

Majima isn't signed by Apple or Microsoft (he's a free fan project), so your computer warns you once.

Windows

  1. Unzip the download and open the MajimaVpet folder.
  2. Double-click MajimaVpet.exe.
  3. If Windows protected your PC appears, click More info, then Run anyway.
  4. His menu is the small icon at the bottom right of the taskbar. If you can't see it, click ^, and drag the icon out to keep it visible.

Mac

  1. Unzip the download and move MajimaVpet.app to Applications.
  2. Open it. If macOS says it can't be opened, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
  4. His menu is the small icon in the menu bar.

Privacy

Majima works completely offline. Your notes and Pomodoro history stay on your computer. To notice when you're typing, he only records when a key was last pressed, never which key. This page has no tracking either.

Update or uninstall

Update: download the new version and replace the old one. Your notes are kept.

Uninstall: quit Majima from his menu and delete the app. To erase your notes too, delete %APPDATA%\MajimaVpet (Windows) or ~/Library/Application Support/MajimaVpet (Mac).

Check your download (optional)

Compare the SHA-256 above with what your computer reports:

Windows (PowerShell): Get-FileHash MajimaVpet-1.0.0-windows.zip
Mac (Terminal): shasum -a 256 MajimaVpet-1.0.0-mac-arm64.zip
