MajimaVpet
==========

A tiny pixel-art Majima that lives on your desktop. He idles and wanders,
dances when you move the mouse, types along with you, keeps a notebook
and runs a Pomodoro timer.

This is an unofficial fan work. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEGA.
Majima and the Like a Dragon / Yakuza series belong to SEGA. Free; not for sale.


Opening it the first time
-------------------------
The app is not signed, so your computer will warn you once.

Windows:  run MajimaVpet.exe. If "Windows protected your PC" appears,
          click "More info", then "Run anyway".
          Majima's menu is the small icon in the bottom-right corner of the
          taskbar (click ^ if it is hidden; you can drag it out to keep it visible).

macOS:    move MajimaVpet.app to Applications, then open it. If macOS says it
          cannot be opened, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
          down and click "Open Anyway". Majima's menu is the small icon in the
          menu bar.


Privacy
-------
Majima works completely offline. Your notes and Pomodoro history stay on
your computer. To notice when you are typing, he only records *when* a key
was last pressed, never which key.


Your notes are kept here (they survive updates and reinstalls)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Windows:  %APPDATA%\MajimaVpet
macOS:    ~/Library/Application Support/MajimaVpet


Updating
--------
Download the new version and replace the old app.


Uninstalling
------------
Quit Majima from his menu, then delete the app (Windows: the MajimaVpet folder).
To also erase your notes, delete the folder listed above.
